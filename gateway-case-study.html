GatewayBrand Launch

A community launch system designed to feel clearer, warmer, and less like traditional real estate.

Role Brand Designer · Visual System
Scope Identity rollout, campaign assets, print, social, email, signage
Client Richcraft Homes
Tools Figma, Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, After Effects
01 · Context

A first home should not feel like a luxury sales pitch.

Gateway was created for younger first-time buyers in Ottawa. The visual language needed to feel modern and approachable while still carrying the trust of an established home builder.

I helped shape the launch rollout across brand applications, campaign touchpoints, digital assets, and sales materials so every piece felt like part of one calm system.

Design Direction
Warm Simple Confident Open

Modern simplicity. Every element earns its place.

02 · Identity System

Clean type, grounded color, and enough space to breathe.

The system was intentionally quiet. Instead of decorative real estate language, the brand relied on proportion, restraint, and consistent hierarchy across every launch touchpoint.

Palette

Warm neutrals and deep green connected the campaign back to the community renderings.

03 · Applications

One system, many surfaces.

The launch needed to move from sales center print pieces to social, email, billboard, and on-site signage without losing its voice.

04 · Outcome

The brand reached the buyers it was built for.

600+

Registrants from the target demographic within the launch window.

4,100+

New users engaging with Gateway brand materials across channels.

35%

Conversion rate from registration interest to confirmed action.

05 · Reflection

The quieter the system, the louder the brand.

I came into this project thinking more options meant more flexibility. By the end, the palette was tighter, the type system was simpler, and white space became one of the strongest parts of the identity.
